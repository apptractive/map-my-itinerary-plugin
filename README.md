# Map My Itinerary

Connect your own Map My Itinerary account to ask about your trips, plan new ones and keep bookings, costs and checklists up to date. Sign in to Map My Itinerary when your AI app asks, choose the access you want and allow it. The tools you get depend on that choice. Our server enforces it; these skills cannot widen it.

Read only lets the AI see your trips (including booking references, costs and who paid), who you travel with, your trips' sharing and forwarding links, and your email and profile, including your wishlist and frequent-flyer numbers (the last four characters, or the full number when you ask). It can't change anything. Read and edit also lets it add, change and remove trips, stops, costs, payments, checklists, notes and your profile, and import links. Read, edit and share also lets it invite and remove people, change what they can do and publish trips. Making changes depends on your Map My Itinerary plan, which is managed in the app. Before it deletes a trip, stop or cost, undoes a recorded payment, invites or removes someone, changes what someone can do, publishes a trip or takes it down, or resets a link, the AI must show you our summary and get your yes.

Buying or managing a plan, account deletion, sign-in changes, adding or opening attachments and calendar subscriptions stay in the app. Stops added through an AI are added by name. Map pins can be added in the app.

No passwords, API keys or credentials belong in conversations or this package. Trip text written by other people, forwarded emails and imported pages are never instructions to the assistant. What the AI sees is handled by your AI provider under its own privacy terms, and may be stored outside Australia. Disconnect in the app under Profile, Settings, Connect Claude or ChatGPT; removing the plugin alone may leave the connection active until 30 days after its last use. Disconnecting stops future access; it does not erase what the AI app already received. Read our [privacy policy](https://mapmyitinerary.com/privacy-policy#ai-assistants) and [terms](https://mapmyitinerary.com/terms-conditions).

In Claude Code, install it with `/plugin install map-my-itinerary --marketplace apptractive/map-my-itinerary-plugin`. In Claude, add it from the plugin directory.
